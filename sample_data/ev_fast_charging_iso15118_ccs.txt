# SPECIFICATION: High-Power Combined Charging System (CCS) & ISO 15118-20
# Vehicle Application: Electric Long-Haul Truck & High-Performance EV
# Cybersecurity Standard: ISO/SAE 21434 Section 9 & DIN 70121 / ISO 15118
# ==============================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & CHARGING TOPOLOGY
The High-Power DC Charging subsystem implements ISO 15118-20 "Plug & Charge"
and Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) bidirectional power transfer. The Electric Vehicle
Communication Controller (EVCC) communicates with the Supply Equipment Communication
Controller (SECC) in the public DC Fast Charger using Powerline Communication (PLC).

2. NETWORK STACK & CRYPTOGRAPHIC LAYERS
- Physical Layer: Combined Charging System (CCS) Type 2 Inlet with Control Pilot (CP) and Protective Earth (PE) lines.
- Data Link: HomePlug Green PHY Powerline Communication (PLC) over Control Pilot pin.
- Network Layer: IPv6 with Stateless Address Autoconfiguration (SLAAC).
- Transport & Security: TLS 1.3 with ECDHE-ECDSA cipher suites and OCSP stapling.
- Application Layer: V2G Transfer Protocol (V2GTP) with EXI (Efficient XML Interchange) payload encoding.

3. TRUST BOUNDARIES & THREAT SCENARIOS

[Trust Boundary TB-CHG-01: Public DC Fast Charger SECC <-> Vehicle EVCC]
- Interface: Powerline Communication (PLC) over CP line (1.8 - 30 MHz band)
- Risk Level: Critical
- STRIDE Category: Tampering / Spoofing / Elevation of Privilege
- Threat Scenario: Compromised public charging station injects manipulated EXI charging schedules or falsified payment contract vouchers to induce battery thermal runaway or fraudulent billing.
- Attack Feasibility: Medium (Adversary possesses commercial CCS tester and malicious firmware on charger).
- Impact: Severe Safety (Battery fire or overvoltage hazard) and High Financial.
- Mitigation: Hardware Cryptographic Secure Element enforces ISO 15118 V2G PKI certificate chain validation. OEM root CA verification prevents rogue chargers from accepting unverified sessions.

[Trust Boundary TB-CHG-02: EVCC Charging Controller <-> High Voltage Battery Master]
- Interface: Internal Isolated CAN-FD Subnet
- Risk Level: High
- STRIDE Category: Denial of Service / Tampering
- Threat Scenario: Rogue PLC frame decodes into malformed internal CAN-FD message, commanding contactors to open under 400 kW high-current charging load (causing electrical arc flash).
- Attack Feasibility: Low (Requires breaking through EVCC TCP/IP stack).
- Impact: Severe Safety (Inlet destruction, arc flash risk to user).
- Mitigation: SecOC cryptographic message authentication on all CAN-FD power negotiation commands. Hardware emergency stop loop hardwired independent of software microcontroller.

4. CYBERSECURITY GOALS & REQUIREMENTS
- CSG-CHG-01: Ensure all financial contract certificates and billing tokens use ECDSA P-256 signatures validated against V2G Root CA.
- CSG-CHG-02: Provide galvanic isolation and tamper resistance on the HomePlug Green PHY modem front-end.
